Pricing playbook & strategy

Best model: value seat + active-company meter

  • $99/seat base + 15 included active companies per seat + $12 per extra active company, outbound as a separate add-on. 15/seat covers a real rep's active pipeline (a 2-seat team gets 30 deals included); the $12 meter then prices each extra deal at ~82% marginal margin, so margin recovers to ~77% as accounts grow. Trade-off: margin troughs at ~54% right at the included edge (a team using all its free deals at the flat seat price) vs ~65% with 10/seat. Generous headroom requires the base seat to fund it — going past ~15/seat at $99 collapses mid-band margin; lift the base to ~$119–149 for a higher floor or 20–25 included.
  • Meter on active companies, never flat per-seat. A flat seat price runs margin-negative on deal-heavy / low-seat accounts — the meter is the safety mechanism that keeps the tail profitable.
  • Cost-plus is a floor, not a price. Use it only as a guardrail; price on value above it.

What drives cost (sensitivity)

  • Active companies × refresh cadence own ~80% of cost — everything else (Slack, meetings, embeddings, storage) is <3% each.
  • Meter price on active companies (the top lever and the clearest value signal); use refresh cadence as your margin throttle, tiered per plan.

Margin levers

  • Route briefs to Haiku before reaching for Max Plan — similar savings (~$45/acct), no Anthropic ToS risk.
  • Let model-price deflation flow to margin — Sonnet is likely to drop ~50%; value pricing pockets that instead of forcing price cuts.
  • Be generous with seats (≈$0 to serve) to drive expansion; charge on deals, not people.

Path to $1M ARR

  • At the proposed prices: ~420 Growth, ~250 Pro, or ~125 Power accounts. Mix-shift toward Pro/Power — the meter makes expansion automatic as customers add pipeline.
